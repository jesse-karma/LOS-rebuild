I. Objective

Overview

Origination Pipeline is Karma's internal tools for originating project financing, a sequence through Due Diligence, Investment Committee (IC) review, Karmapreneur (KP) term-sheet agreement, Finance allocation, Legal contracting, and Disbursement. Its purpose is to replace Coda as the predecessor system.

Pipeline sits alongside CRM, Monitoring, and Credit Quality as capabilities within KIT (Karma's Internal Tools), the single platform Karma is building incrementally to replace today's disconnected tools. The Loan Management System (LMS) is the only external system: after disbursement. This PRD focuses on the Origination Pipeline; it describes the required capabilities and integration between systems.

Why We Build It

  • Coda does not provide the development, testing, logging, or flexibility needed to improve the origination process
  • Important credit controls such as concentration limits are currently manual or formula-based. Pipeline will make these controls visible at the right time.
  • Origination is still heavily manual. A purpose-built workflow is needed to support higher project volume without losing consistency or auditability.

Objective & Key Results

Origination in one system for moving a financing request from Due Diligence to Disbursement, with the right permissions at each stage and a complete history of important decisions.

  • KR1: 100% of new financing requests are created and progressed in these internal tools.
  • KR2: All users (Analysts, IC members, Finance, and Legal users work in this tool for new and existing projects, without parallel Coda use.
  • KR3: Every IC decision, Finance split, Legal sign-off, and disbursement records the actor, time, and reason or note where relevant.
  • KR4: Pipeline captures entry and exit times for each stage so Karma can measure the median Due Diligence-to-Disbursement cycle time after launch.

Scope

In Scope

  • The full Pipeline lifecycle: Due Diligence → IC Review → KP Review → Finance Split → Legal Agreement → Finance Disbursement.
  • Permissions for Analyst, IC, Finance, and Legal users, including users with more than one role.
  • The submission form: its common sections, conditional sections, and stage-based editing rules.
  • Pipeline records for Projects, KPs, Contacts, PTs, Payors, Invoices/POs, notes, tasks, and Tranches.
  • Integration between CRM, Monitoring, LMS seamlessly with single source database

II. Capability

Roles & Access

Roles & Permissions

People can hold more than one role. They use one active role at a time, and may switch only to roles assigned to them. For this release, a person who holds both Analyst and IC roles may review and vote on their own team's submission.

RoleWhat they can doBoundaries
Analyst Create and complete a submission in Due Diligence; submit it to IC; edit Conditions before submission; revise and resubmit a Project returned by IC; request or edit a planned Tranche. After submission to IC, all Analyst-editable fields are locked. Analysts cannot take IC, Finance, Legal, or Disbursement actions.
Investment Committee Approve a Project or send it back to the Analyst with a required reason; edit Conditions Precedent and Subsequent during IC Review. A send-back is not a permanent rejection; the Analyst may revise and resubmit. IC cannot take Finance, Legal, or Analyst actions.
Finance Set the KF/KCF allocation, send a valid allocation to Legal, and confirm an eligible disbursement. Finance cannot change upstream deal information or Legal records. A split cannot be changed after it is sent to Legal.
Legal Confirm that the master agreement is signed and issue a disbursement note for each Tranche. Legal cannot change upstream deal information or Finance allocations.

Authentication

User will use Google Workspace sign-in. Until it is implemented, the current build uses a simulated signed-in user and only shows the roles assigned to that person.

Data Model

This object model is the shared foundation for KIT: CRM and future Monitoring and Credit Quality modules are expected to build on the same objects and relationships rather than maintain separate schemas.

The Project is the central record: it is a financing request, sometimes also called a Card or submission. Other records belong to either the Project or its Karmapreneur.

Object Relationships

  • A KP may have many Projects, Contacts, and PTs.
  • A Project may have many Tranches, except Asset B, which allows one disbursement only.
  • Asset B Projects use an Invoice or PO linked to a Payor.
  • Notes record Project or KP activity; Tasks record Project or Contact follow-ups.

Project Naming & Numbering

Every Project has two identifiers that behave differently: a mutable display name set during Due Diligence, and an immutable sequence number assigned only once the Project is disbursed.

  • Project Name: Brand Name plus Project Name (free text — a branch, the project itself, or anything the Analyst chooses). Editable throughout Due Diligence; locks when the Project is submitted to IC Review, along with the other Analyst-editable fields.
  • Sequence Number: assigned automatically the moment a Project records its first disbursement. The number is the count of that Karmapreneur's already-disbursed Projects, plus one — their 1st, 2nd, 3rd disbursed Project. A Project that is rejected, expires, or is cancelled before ever disbursing never receives a number, so no gaps are created.
  • All Tranches under one Project share that Project's single sequence number; a Tranche does not receive its own.
  • Because the number reflects actual disbursement order rather than creation or submission order, two Projects for the same Karmapreneur can receive numbers out of the order they were created in.

System Integration & Scope Boundary

CRM passes a qualified lead into Pipeline. Pipeline owns origination until a disbursement is recorded. Monitoring becomes the internal-tools view for post-disbursement activity, while LMS becomes the servicing system of record.

CRM → Pipeline

CRM and Pipeline are being built in parallel. CRM hands off a qualified lead once it reaches the Due Diligence stage; the handoff requires only a Brand Name, and passes every other field CRM already has as optional. Project Name is optional at handoff but must be filled in before the Analyst can submit to IC.

Pipeline → Monitoring

Not every disbursed Project becomes visible to Monitoring — only the subset that matches one of Monitoring's tracked capabilities, evaluated from the Project's first disbursement onward:

  • Overdues Monitoring: flags a Project that is overdue.
  • Branch Opening: tracks Asset A in particular, from disbursement to its due date.
  • PVA Monitoring: tracks projected vs. actual for a branch or Project using a revenue-share scheme.
  • Monitoring Visit: reminds Karma that a KP hasn't been monitored, visited, or contacted.

Regardless of whether a Project surfaces in any of these, every confirmed disbursement is always reflected in LMS.

Pipeline → LMS

Pipeline sends a disbursement event for every confirmed Project or Tranche disbursement. The delivery is asynchronous: Pipeline records the decision immediately, then retries LMS delivery with backoff if needed. A failed event remains visible for manual retry.

Asset Classes A, B-I, B-PO, and D are in scope. Submission Type must include Project, Plafond, or both; Buffer may be added but cannot stand alone. Asset A is Project-only. Asset B allows only one disbursement. Asset B pricing-floor issues are warnings, not hard blocks.

Submission Type Field Rules

A submission can be Project-only, Plafond-only, or Project and Plafond together. Buffer is only selectable when Plafond is selected, and is cleared automatically if Plafond is turned off; Buffer cannot stand alone.

  • Always required, regardless of Submission Type: Project Description, Financing Use.
  • Always shown, optional when Project is not included: Project Credit Memo, Notes, Other — this is the shared memo/notes container for either a Project or a Plafond proposal, so it must stay available even on a Plafond-only submission.
  • Shown only when Project is included: the pricing Calculator, Requested Project Amount, Karma Carry / Monitoring Fee.
  • Funding Source (KF / KCF / KS vs. Members) is not part of this form. It is decided later, at Finance Split (see Story 3), for both Project and Plafond submissions.

Asset Class Rules

Asset Class is A, B-I, B-PO, or D. Selecting Plafond removes Asset Class A from the options — Asset A is Project-only. Asset Class drives both the allowed Financing Type and the allowed Financing Use options.

Field Completeness & Locking

  • A Project can be submitted only when all fields that apply to its type and asset class are complete
  • Once submitted, all Analyst-editable fields — including the Project Name — lock. During IC Review, IC may edit only Conditions Precedent and Subsequent
  • Warnings — concentration, exposure, pricing-floor, and similar — appear inline next to the field or section they relate to. There is no single consolidated warnings panel; Analysts can view them, IC can act on them

Concentration Limits

Karma runs two separate concentration-limit regimes, and both are checked against a Project or Plafond's full requested (or approved) amount — not a KF/KCF-split portion. The KF/KCF split is decided later, manually by Finance, based on each entity's real-time bank balance at the time of disbursement or Tranche; it is not known at submission or IC Review time, so every Project or Plafond is checked against both regimes regardless of how it is eventually funded. Future improvement: automatically fetch each entity's balance and suggest the KF/KCF split, rather than relying on Finance to check and enter it manually.

These are warnings, not automatic blocks: IC may still approve after seeing the warning, except where a Stretch Maximum requires the entire IC to approve (see below).

KarmaFood (Karma's own balance-sheet entity) — denominated as a percent of Karma's total consolidated equity (Net Assets), effective 7 July 2025, reset and adjusted every quarter.

LimitTier% of Net AssetsRp (as of May 2025 Net Assets)

EntrepreneurNormal Maximum5%Rp 10.1 billion
Project / EntrepreneurStretch Maximum — requires the entire IC to approve10%Rp 20.2 billion

Payor Exposure Limit

Pipeline must also warn IC when combined exposure to the same Payor exceeds the Brand-limit threshold. This is always a warning, never a hard block, because appropriate exposure differs by Payor type.

Exposure is calculated from approved amounts and is released when the financing is fully repaid. Pipeline will need a repayment update from Monitoring or LMS to keep this calculation current after servicing begins.

Open item: a planned Tranche that Finance never disburses has no release trigger in this release — it continues to reserve exposure indefinitely. This should be revisited once Tranche resolution beyond "stays pending" is defined.

User Stories & Acceptance Criteria

Story 1 — Analyst: Submit a Project for IC Review

Story: As an Analyst, I want to submit a complete Project to IC so the committee can make a financing decision.

  • Analysts can edit applicable fields in Due Diligence.
  • Submission requires all applicable fields and a valid Submission Type.
  • Submission moves the Project to IC Review and locks Analyst-editable fields.
  • A Project cannot be submitted if neither Project nor Plafond is selected.

Key scenarios: A complete Project enters IC Review once; a repeated submission does not create a duplicate transition; an invalid Submission Type is blocked with a clear explanation.

Story 2 — IC: Decide on a submitted Project

Story: As an IC member, I want to approve or send back a submitted Project so only appropriately reviewed financing requests proceed.

IC Approval Logic: There are three IC members and no member hierarchy. A request of up to and including Rp4B needs one approval; a request above Rp4B and up to and including Rp6B needs two; a request above Rp6B needs three. The threshold is determined by the Project's requested amount or the Plafond total, whichever applies — neither a Project nor a Plafond request is exempt from this check. Each member can approve once per decision round and cannot change or withdraw. One IC send-back immediately returns the Project to Due Diligence and must include a note.

IC Decision Rules:

  • IC has two outcomes: approve, or send back to Due Diligence. A negative IC decision never permanently closes a Project
  • A KP counter starts a new decision round. Previous IC votes are reset and the full quorum is required again
  • The system keeps a history of each decision round, including votes and send-back notes
  • IC can approve once the required voting threshold is met.
  • One IC member can send a Project back with a required note.
  • A send-back returns the Project to Due Diligence and the Analyst can resubmit it.
  • IC can edit Conditions during IC Review.
  • An Analyst-only user cannot take an IC action.

Key scenarios: A two-vote Project proceeds after two distinct approvals; one send-back returns it to Due Diligence; a KP counter restarts the IC decision round.

Story 3 — Finance: Split approved financing into KF/KCF

Story: As a Finance user, I want to allocate an approved amount between KF and KCF so Legal can prepare the agreement.

Split Basis: For now, Finance determines the KF/KCF split manually, based on each entity's real-time bank balance at the time of this disbursement or Tranche. Future improvement: automatically fetch each entity's balance and suggest a split.

  • Finance can enter the KF/KCF amounts in Finance Split.
  • The two amounts must equal the approved amount before Finance can send the Project to Legal.
  • An invalid split cannot be sent to Legal.
  • Non-Finance users cannot edit the allocation.

Key scenarios: A valid split proceeds to Legal; an invalid split clearly shows that the total does not match.

Story 4 — Legal: Confirm contracts signed

Story: As a Legal user, I want to confirm the master agreement is signed so the Project can proceed to disbursement.

  • Legal can confirm signing in Legal Agreement.
  • Confirmation moves the Project to Waiting for Disbursement.
  • One sign-off is sufficient for this release; document checklists and e-signature tracking are not required.
  • Non-Legal users cannot confirm signing.

Story 5 — Finance: Disburse funds

Story: As a Finance user, I want to confirm a completed disbursement so the KP receives financing and servicing can begin.

  • Finance can disburse once the Project is Waiting for Disbursement.
  • Pipeline records the disbursement, shows the Project in Monitoring, and sends an LMS event.
  • Plafond-headroom validation is automated before disbursement.
  • A non-Finance user cannot disburse.

Story 6 — Analyst: Request a Tranche

Story: As an Analyst, I want to request a new Tranche on an Active Project so the KP can receive the next portion of financing.

Tranche Rules: A Tranche is a partial payout from one approved Project. It uses the same IC decision, KP-accepted term sheet, and master legal agreement; it is not a new financing request. For example, a Rp3B approved Project may be paid as three Rp1B Tranches over time.

  • A Project's first disbursement is its Tranche 1, using the same Finance Split → Legal → Ready for Disbursement → Disbursed flow as every later Tranche — there is no separate mechanism for a Project's first payout.
  • Finance completes a new KF/KCF split for every Tranche.
  • Only Tranche 1's Legal step includes signing the master agreement. Every Tranche's Legal step includes issuing a disbursement note; the master agreement is never re-signed after Tranche 1.
  • The sum of a Project's completed and planned Tranches cannot exceed its approved amount. This is a hard block, not a warning. Asset D's revolving Plafond is the exception, since repayments restore headroom there.
  • The Project shows both the remaining balance and a list of planned and completed Tranches.
  • Pipeline sends one LMS event for each confirmed Tranche.
  • Asset B is always single-disbursement. A second request is blocked.
  • Asset A may use Tranches but does not revolve after repayment.
  • Asset D may use Tranches. Its Plafond portion can revolve as repayments restore headroom; its Project portion does not.
  • There is no limit on the number or timing of Tranches for Asset A or D.
  • A Tranche shares its Project's sequence number; it does not receive its own (see Project Naming & Numbering under Data Model).
  • Finance is the sole gate for disbursement. If a Tranche should not proceed — for example, the KP becomes unresponsive — Finance simply does not disburse it. There is no cancellation action in this release; an un-disbursed Tranche stays pending indefinitely.
  • Analyst can request a Tranche once the Project is Active (Asset A or D only).
  • Analyst can edit a planned Tranche before Finance completes its split.
  • A Tranche cannot be requested if it would push the Project's total Tranches over the approved amount, unless Asset D's revolving Plafond headroom allows it.
  • An Asset B Project cannot request a second Tranche.

Key scenarios: A Tranche within remaining headroom is accepted; a Tranche that would exceed the approved amount is blocked with a clear explanation; a second Tranche request on an Asset B Project is blocked.

Story 7 — Finance & Legal: Process and disburse a Tranche

Story: As a Finance user, I want to split and disburse a requested Tranche so the KP receives the next payout and servicing stays current.

Tranche States:

StatusOwnerMeaning
Finance SplitFinanceThe Analyst has requested a Tranche and Finance must allocate KF/KCF.
LegalLegalFinance has completed the allocation; Legal issues the disbursement note (and, for a Project's first Tranche only, signs the master agreement).
Ready for DisbursementFinanceAllocation and Legal notes are complete.
DisbursedFinance / systemThe transfer is confirmed and Pipeline has created the LMS event.
  • Finance can enter the KF/KCF amounts for the Tranche.
  • The two amounts must equal the Tranche amount before it can move to Legal.
  • Legal issues a disbursement note for the Tranche, and signs the master agreement only if it is the Project's first Tranche.
  • Finance can disburse once the Tranche is Ready for Disbursement; Pipeline records it and sends one LMS event.
  • Finance is not required to disburse on any particular timeline — an un-disbursed Tranche simply stays pending, with no forced cancellation.

Key scenarios: A valid Tranche split proceeds to Legal and disburses; an invalid split is blocked; a Tranche that never gets disbursed (for example, the KP becomes unresponsive) stays pending indefinitely.
